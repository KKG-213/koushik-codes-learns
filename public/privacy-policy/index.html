Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how Koushik web solutions handles information when you visit the portfolio website or contact the business, including through WhatsApp.

Who operates this service

The portfolio website and the business messaging integration are operated by Koushik Karmakar, doing business as Koushik web solutions. For privacy questions or requests, contact razkmr1234@gmail.com or call +91 6200067891.

Information from the portfolio website

The portfolio does not currently provide a website contact form or account registration. Its contact links open your email application, phone dialer, or WhatsApp. Information you choose to include in a message—such as your name, contact details, inquiry, and any attachments—is sent through the service you choose, rather than submitted to a form on this website.

Vercel hosts the website and may process technical request information needed to deliver it. The site code contains no configured analytics or advertising tracking. Vercel's own infrastructure and privacy terms govern its processing.

WhatsApp messages

When you contact the business on WhatsApp, information can include your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name when available, messages and attachments you send, replies, and message-related metadata such as timestamps, message identifiers, and delivery status. Meta and WhatsApp process information to provide the messaging service and Cloud API.

The business messaging integration uses the WhatsApp Cloud API and self-hosted n8n workflows running on a Google Cloud Compute Engine virtual machine. Those workflows may process conversation content and related metadata to receive, route, manage, and respond to business inquiries and service conversations, and to maintain the messaging workflow.

How information is used

Information shared with the business is used to understand and respond to your inquiry, communicate about requested services, and operate or troubleshoot the related messaging workflow. This policy does not describe use for advertising or unrelated profiling.

Service providers

  • Vercel hosts the portfolio website and may handle technical data associated with website requests.
  • Meta / WhatsApp provides WhatsApp and the WhatsApp Cloud API used for business messages.
  • Google Cloud provides the Compute Engine virtual machine on which the business hosts n8n.
  • n8n is the self-hosted workflow software used to process messaging workflows. Email and phone providers also process communications according to the service you use.

These providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy practices. The business does not control provider-side retention or infrastructure logs.

Retention

No fixed retention period has been verified for this notice. Business messages may remain in the relevant email, WhatsApp, or workflow systems while needed to handle the conversation. Workflow and hosting logs, backups, and provider records may have separate retention settings or periods. Contact the business to ask for a review or deletion of information it can identify and control.

Security

The portfolio website is served over HTTPS. The security and access settings for email, Meta/WhatsApp, n8n, and the Google Cloud virtual machine depend on their configured accounts and services. No particular encryption-at-rest, backup, or account-access configuration is represented here. Please avoid sending highly sensitive information through ordinary email or messaging.

Your choices

You can choose whether to contact the business and which available channel to use. If you do not want to use WhatsApp, contact the business by email or phone instead. You may also use WhatsApp's own settings and controls for your account and conversations.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of a website inquiry or WhatsApp interaction, email razkmr1234@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request.” Identify the email address or WhatsApp number used and, if possible, the approximate date and channel of the conversation. Do not include passwords or access tokens.

Koushik web solutions will review the request manually, locate records it can identify, and delete information under its control where it is appropriate to do so. This is not an automated deletion process. Copies held by Meta/WhatsApp, Vercel, Google Cloud, email providers, or in provider-controlled logs and backups may be subject to those providers' processes and retention settings; this policy does not promise their deletion or a specific completion time. You may need to contact the relevant provider for data it controls.
